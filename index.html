Simulated first

Every transaction runs on mainnet in simulation before your wallet sees it. If it would fail, you see why instead.

Your keys only

AFK builds the transaction with the official PumpSwap SDK. Your wallet signs it. AFK never holds keys or funds.

Fits in one

Address lookup tables squeeze swap and deposit into a single transaction. If a pool needs more room, AFK splits it in two.

Lands fast

Compute and priority fees come from live network data for that pool, not a guess.

07 — What AFK can't do

Read this part awake.

  • Prices move. When the coin's price changes, your position changes with it. If the coin halves, a position is worth about 71% of what it was before fees. That's impermanent loss, and fees only offset it if trading stays busy.
  • Coins can die. A pump.fun coin can lose almost all of its value. Liquidity in it goes down with it.
  • Fees follow trading. "Earns / day" is the last 24 hours. Quiet days pay less.
  • Third-party program. Your liquidity sits in PumpSwap, pump.fun's AMM. AFK doesn't control it.

09 — FAQ

Questions, answered while you're away.

What is AFK?

A front end for adding liquidity to pump.fun's PumpSwap pools in one click. You put in SOL, AFK turns it into a liquidity position in the pool you picked, and every trade in that pool pays you a cut.

Where do the earnings come from?

Each PumpSwap trade pays a fee. On most graduated coins 0.20% of every trade goes to the pool's liquidity providers, and on very small coins it's 0.02%. The rest goes to PumpSwap and the coin's creator. Your share of the LP fee is your share of the pool.

Do I need to claim anything?

No. The LP fee stays inside the pool, so each LP token is backed by a little more SOL and coin after every trade. You collect when you withdraw.

Can I lose money?

Yes. If the coin's price moves, your position moves with it, and a coin that dumps takes its liquidity down too. The earnings table shows the price-move math before you add anything.

Is it custodial?

No. The server builds and simulates the transaction, your wallet signs it, and the LP tokens land in your wallet. AFK never sees a private key.

What does AFK charge?

Nothing. You pay Solana's network fees and a small refundable rent for new token accounts. Swaps inside the one-click deposit pay PumpSwap's normal trade fee.

Which pools work?

Every pump.fun coin's canonical PumpSwap pool that's paired with SOL. Coins still on the bonding curve have no pool yet.

Does it work on my phone?

Open the site inside Phantom's or Solflare's in-app browser, then connect.
